Changes made in this derivative
-------------------------------

FARMWISE converted each source parcel polygon to an interior representative
point, transformed coordinates from EPSG:3035 to EPSG:4326, concatenated the
regional stack tables, retained the annual cYYYY and cfYYYY attributes, and
serialized the result as CSV or compressed CSV.GZ. Invalid or empty geometries
are omitted and counted in PROVENANCE.json.
